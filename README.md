### About the Game

Vacuform is a cozy couch co-op exploration base-building game where you and your friend vacuum up the corruption that has overgrown a planet once inhabited by humans. Explore different biomes, vacuum up enemies, resources, and find schematics to build and customize your base.

The game was designed and developed from start to finish in 9 weeks by a team consisting of 10 programmers, 15 graphics artists, and 5 game designers.

The game can be downloaded from itch.io [here](https://blushytears.itch.io/vacuform)

### Tech

- Made in Unreal Engine 5.4
- Programmed in Angelscript
- Trunk-based version control in Perforce
